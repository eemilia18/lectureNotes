How to prove two propositional logic statements are 
equivalent?

two truth tables that have the same output for every possible input, 

deduction from both sides 


How to prove propositional logic statements are 
NOT equivalent?

find one truth assignment where the outputs differ 

can't prove not equivalent with deduction
